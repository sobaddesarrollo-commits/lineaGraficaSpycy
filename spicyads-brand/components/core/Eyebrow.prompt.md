Mono, ALL-CAPS, wide-tracked kicker with a spice dot — opens nearly every section. The most recognizable SpicyAds typographic move.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Servicios</Eyebrow>
<h2 className="display">Más leads, menos humo</h2>
```

`tone="muted"` on busy backgrounds; `dot={false}` to drop the gradient dot. Always uppercase, short (1–3 words).
