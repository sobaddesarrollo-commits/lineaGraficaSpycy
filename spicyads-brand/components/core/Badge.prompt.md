Compact pill for status, category, or metric labels.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" variant="soft">Activa</Badge>
<Badge tone="spice" variant="solid">Nuevo</Badge>
```

Tones: `spice | neutral | success | warning | info`. `soft` (tinted bg) for most uses; `solid` for high-emphasis tags. Keep to 1–2 words.
